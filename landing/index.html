Frequently asked questions

What is Dexcrate?

Dexcrate is a web app for Pokémon TCG collectors. It keeps your collection, shows every set as a binder with what you own and what is missing, and shows the Cardmarket market price of each card and of your whole collection.

Which Pokémon cards does Dexcrate support?

English sets and Asian sets (Japanese, Chinese and Korean) from the TCGdex card database, including promos. Cards that are not in the database can still be added by hand.

Where do the card prices come from?

Market prices in euros come from Cardmarket via TCGdex and are refreshed daily. Every card has a direct link to its Cardmarket page.

Can I scan my cards?

Yes. Take a photo of a single card, a whole binder page or a quick series of cards, and Dexcrate recognises the name, set and number. You check the result before anything is added.

Does Dexcrate work on my phone?

Yes. Dexcrate runs in the browser and can be installed on your home screen like an app. Your collection stays viewable when you are offline.
