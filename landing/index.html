Everything a card collector needs

From the first scan to the last missing card in a set: Dexcrate keeps track, so you can enjoy collecting.

  • Scan cards with your camera

    Photograph a single card, a whole binder page or a quick series. Dexcrate reads the name, set and number; you confirm before anything is added.

  • Every set as a binder

    See which cards you own and which are missing, page by page in 6- or 9-pocket view, with your progress and the cost to complete the set.

  • Cardmarket prices

    The market price of every card and the value of your whole collection, with price history and a direct link to the card on Cardmarket.

  • Wishlist and smart lists

    Keep the cards you are hunting for, filter by price, rarity, type and set, and save filters as lists you can open with one tap.

  • Purchases and sales

    Record what you paid and what you sold for, including shipping and fees, and see your profit or loss per card and overall.

  • Works like an app

    Install Dexcrate on your home screen. Your collection stays viewable offline, with a light and a dark theme and backups you can download.

How it works

  1. Add your cards

  3. Know what it is worth

    Daily Cardmarket prices show the value of each card and of everything you own.

Frequently asked questions

What is Dexcrate?

Dexcrate is a web app for Pokémon TCG collectors. It keeps your collection, shows every set as a binder with what you own and what is missing, and shows the Cardmarket market price of each card and of your whole collection.

Which Pokémon cards does Dexcrate support?

English sets and Asian sets (Japanese, Chinese and Korean) from the TCGdex card database, including promos. Cards that are not in the database can still be added by hand.

Where do the card prices come from?

Market prices in euros come from Cardmarket via TCGdex and are refreshed daily. Every card has a direct link to its Cardmarket page.

Can I scan my cards?

Yes. Take a photo of a single card, a whole binder page or a quick series of cards, and Dexcrate recognises the name, set and number. You check the result before anything is added.

Does Dexcrate work on my phone?

Yes. Dexcrate runs in the browser and can be installed on your home screen like an app. Your collection stays viewable when you are offline.

Can I sign up?

Not yet. Dexcrate is in a private beta and new sign-ups are closed for now.

Already have an account?

Open Dexcrate in your browser, or install it on your phone from the app's menu.
